### b)

x^3 + 5x^2

### c)

$$
x^2 - 2x + 1
$$

#### Apliquemos Bhaskara

$$
x = \frac{2 \pm \sqrt{4 - 4}}{2}
$$

$$
\to x = \frac{2}{2} = 1 \text{ doble}
$$

$$
(x-1)(x-1)
$$

### d)

$$
x^2 - 4x + 3
$$

$$
x = \frac{4 \pm \sqrt{(-4)^2 - 4(1)(3)}}{2(1)}
$$

$$
x = \frac{4 \pm \sqrt{16 - 12}}{2}
$$

$$
\frac{4 + 4}{2} = 2
$$

$$
\frac{4 - 4}{2} = 0
$$

$$
(x-2)(x-2)
$$

### e)

$$
6x^2 + 17x + 12
$$

$$
x = \frac{-17 \pm \sqrt{17^2 - 4(6)(12)}}{2(6)}
$$

$$
x = \frac{-17 \pm \sqrt{289 - 288}}{12}
$$

$$
x = \frac{-17 \pm \sqrt{1}}{12}
$$

$$
\frac{-17 - 1}{12} = \frac{-18}{12} = \frac{-3}{2}
$$

$$
\frac{-17 + 1}{12} = \frac{-16}{12} = -\frac{-4}{3}
$$

$$
6\left(x+\frac{-3}{2}\right)\left(x+\frac{-4}{3}\right)
$$
