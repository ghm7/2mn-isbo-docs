## VIII)

$$
3(a+b)(a-b)=3a^2-3b^2
$$

$$
(3a+3b)(a-b)
$$

$$
3a^2-3ab+3ba-3b^2
$$

$$
3a^2-3b^2
$$

Si $(a=4; \ b=3)$

$$
\begin{aligned}
3(4+3)(4-3) &= 3(4)^2-3(3)^2 \\
(12+9)(4-3) &= 3(16)-3(9) \\
48-36+36-27 &= 48-27 \\
21 &= 21
\end{aligned}
$$

Si $(a=5; \ b=2)$

$$
\begin{aligned}
3(5+2)(5-2) &= 3(5)^2-3(2)^2 \\
(15+6)(5-2) &= 3(25)-3(4) \\
75-30+30-12 &= 75-12 \\
63 &= 63
\end{aligned}
$$

Identidad

$$
c(a+b)(a-b)=ca^2-cb^2
$$

$$
\forall a\in\mathbb{R};\ b\in\mathbb{R}\ |\ (a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2
$$

$$
k(a+b)(a-b)=ka^2-kb^2
$$

$$
k\in\mathbb{R}
$$

> [!NOTE]
>
> $(k)$ es un escalar.
