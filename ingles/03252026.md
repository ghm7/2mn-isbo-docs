# Simple past tense: Grammar structure

## To be

### +

- I **was**
- You **were**
- He **was**
- She **was**
- It **was**
- We **were**
- They **were**

### ?

- **Was** I
- **Were** You
- **Was** He
- **Was** She
- **Was** It
- **Were** We
- **Were** They

### -

- I **was not/wasn't**
- You **were not/weren't**
- He **was not/wasn't**
- She **was not/wasn't**
- It **was not/wasn't**
- We **were not/weren't**
- They **were not/weren't**

### +

- I **went**

- **did** He go

- **did** They go

### -
