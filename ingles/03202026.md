Thomas J. Watson Senior began his business career as a sewing machine salesman
and later when he became president of IBM, he used his sales techniques to
promote the company´s name.

When Watson joined IBM in 1914, the company was almost broke. Under his
leadership Watson believed that the way to win a person´s loyalty is to build up
the person´s self-respect. So under his management, IBM employees earned quite
good salaries and good pensions. In return, he demanded loyalty and enthusiasm.

Answer the questions.

1.  Who was Thomas Watson?
2.  What did he use to promote a company?
3.  When did he join the company?
4.  What did he believe?
5.  What happened with employees during his management period?

---

1. He was the president of IBM.
2. He used his sales techniques.
3. He joined in 1914.
4. He believed that the way to win a person's loyalty is to build up the
   person's self-respect or in other words he payed quite good salaries.
5. They earned really good salaries.
