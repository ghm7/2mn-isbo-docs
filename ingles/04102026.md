# Finding out what happened: Product launch

You are working for an IT company and this company launched a new electronic device last year.

You want to write an article about it for your company magazine. First, write some questions to get the information you need.

| Topic                         | Question                      |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Type of product               | What type of ... ... you ...? |
| Time it took to develop       | How long ... it ... to ... ?  |
| Size of investment            | How much ... you ... ?        |
| Advertising media             | Where ... you ... it ?        |
| Sales in the first two months | How many units ... you ... ?  |

- A - Did, sell?
- B - Did, advertise
- C - Did, take, develop
- D - Did, invest
- E - Product, did, launch

## Place the right words in the sentences

- What type of product did you launch?
- How long did it take to develop?
- How much did you invest?
- Where did you advertise it?
- How many units did you sell?

## Answer the questions

- The product that I launched was a video game
- It took me three months to develop
- I invested one million dollars
- I advertised it on Facebook and Instagram
- I sold five million units
